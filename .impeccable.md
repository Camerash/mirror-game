# Design Context

Players explore a calm spatial puzzle, primarily on iPhone and iPad, with Android support and possible PC release. Touch input must be as complete as mouse and keyboard input.

The tone is calm, curious, and warm. References supplied by the user include Monument Valley, Viewfinder, and Journey. Use simple 3D architectural forms, a fixed orthographic camera, faded painted surfaces, and clear walking edges.

Distinguish warm original structures, cooler reflections, and patterned absolutes. Use a restrained ivory and ink interface. Keep the stage central, preview consequences clearly, and make experiments easy to undo. Reflow controls and camera framing around safe areas; do not shrink a desktop layout onto a phone.
