Frame 4, the glimpse: on the ice she holds the small oval hand mirror up in front of her with her mittened left hand. A tall pane of frost stands beside her, and in it her twin sister appears pale and translucent, the mirror image of her (mitten on the RIGHT hand), waving. In the reflection only, a trail of glowing pale-blue footprints continues off the pond across the far bank toward the edge of the snowfield, where there are no footprints in the real snow.
