Polished porcelain and ceramic. The original blocks are warm ivory glazed ceramic with fine crackle and shallow carved arch reliefs on their sides. The mirror's copy is cool, slightly translucent blue-white porcelain. The goal block is carved green jade. The mirror has a thin warm brass frame and nearly clear glass with a soft pearl-blue glow along its edges. Soft dawn light, a gentle mauve-to-peach gradient background, delicate soft shadows and ambient occlusion, a few drifting petals. Like a museum ceramic diorama: elegant, serene, refined.
