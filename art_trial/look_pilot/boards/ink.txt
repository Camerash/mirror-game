Paper and ink. The world is cut from thick off-white washi paper and card, its edges drawn with fine sumi ink lines, with soft watercolour washes in indigo and pale grey and one vermilion accent (the goal ring and a ribbon on the gate). The mirror's copy is painted in translucent blue ink wash, like a reflection brushed onto rice paper. A paper-textured background with faint ink-wash mountains far behind. Calm and poetic, in the spirit of East Asian ink painting.
