Sunlit terracotta. The original blocks are whitewashed plaster over terracotta, with blue painted trims and small potted herbs; the mirror's copy is the same architecture rendered in pale sea-blue glass; the goal block is turquoise tile mosaic. Warm late-afternoon sun with long crisp shadows, a deep clear sky gradient, soft clouds drifting below the islands and a hint of sea far below. Warm and nostalgic: a summer town remembered.
