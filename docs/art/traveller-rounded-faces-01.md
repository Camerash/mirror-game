## Direction and review

The user preferred RiME, with Jusant secondary, after reviewing image references. The earlier human faces felt too realistic; the minimal faces felt too cartoonish. These new proposals use rounded facial structure, visible eyelids, a simple nose and mouth, and broad matte shading. They do not reuse the old blank-face traveller.

Reviewed three busts and their full-body views: face placement inside the hood, cheek overlap, connected neck, visible boots, clear labels, and matched costume were acceptable for concept selection. A has fuller cheeks; B has balanced proportions; C has a longer face and firmer jaw. Eye size remains larger than the RiME reference and is not approved. Full-body character heights vary with the age/proportion study, despite the prompt's common-height request. The board is not a scale or multi-view construction reference. Simplify cloth grain and confirm the selected character's shape in the later model sheet.

Reference pages: [RiME character art](https://www.polygon.com/2017/1/3/14154646/rime-gameplay-trailer-2017-ps4-nintendo-switch-xbox-pc), [Jusant screenshot](https://www.gamesradar.com/jusant-review/). These guide the amount of facial detail, not character identity or costume.

No runtime tests, exports, or Blender changes were made for this image study.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Make an original character face exploration board for a calm magical puzzle game with a soft ceramic world. A restrained stylized 3D illustration, like a small beautifully hand-shaped game model, not photorealism. Six drawings arranged in THREE COLUMNS: each column has one large head-and-shoulders bust above one smaller matching full-body figure. Column labels exactly "A — Gentle", "B — Quiet", "C — Mature". Simple small typography. Landscape board on a plain light warm-grey background, equal spacing. All heads and bodies use the SAME three-quarter camera direction, looking slightly toward viewer's left. Soft diffuse light, matte materials, broad deliberate light/shadow planes. No scenery or platforms.

Core facial style: think the degree of simplified sculptural anatomy seen in RiME, with the restrained features of Jusant as secondary direction, but create an ORIGINAL identity and clothing, do not reproduce either game's protagonist. Rounded cheek and jaw volumes, modest small almond-shaped eyes with visible upper lids, a simple sculpted wedge nose, and a small understated mouth. Eyes have subdued dark irises and small off-white sclera, not black dot eyes, oversized Disney/Pixar eyes, anime eyes, sparkling doll eyes or prominent lashes. Facial rendering is VERY SIMPLE: no pores, realistic skin translucency, realistic lip gloss, microtextures, detailed ear anatomy, or airbrushed beauty portrait. Nose and cheeks should be readable broad shapes. A calm neutral expression, with the subtle personality changes below.

Each figure wears the same plain desaturated blue-grey cloth hood and a mid-calf cloak with a simple front opening, darker plain trousers and simple rounded dark boots. The hood drapes naturally around a head, connected to the shoulders. Face is part of a rounded head INSIDE the hood; the far hood rim slightly occludes the far cheek. There is room between skin and the fabric rim; no projecting face plate, no rigid oval collar around face, no floating mask. Warm medium tan skin and simple dark brown hair shape, just a small forehead lock, no elaborate curls or individual strands. Hands mostly tucked inside cloak; no props, belts, jewellery, emblems, trim, embroidery, patterned hem or accessories. Broad simple cloth folds. Clearly distinct from a triangular black mascot or hat-like cowl.
Use the identical hood/costume colours, pose, lighting, camera angle and rendering for all columns. Each lower full-body figure must match its own bust exactly. Figures share a common floor baseline and equal overall visual height.

A GENTLE: youthful but not babyish, slightly fuller cheeks, soft rounded jaw, small natural eyes and a very faint warm expression. Compact proportions around five heads tall.
B QUIET: age-neutral young adult, balanced head proportions, less cheek fullness than A, soft but clearer nose bridge, relaxed small eyes and neutral thoughtful expression. Around five and a half heads tall.
C MATURE: adult, slightly longer face, a broader simpler nose and subtly firmer jaw; composed expression. Around six heads tall. No wrinkles or facial hair, no stern warrior look.

Emphasize the middle ground between realistic animated-film anatomy and flat emoji-like faces. Keep geometry plausible and simple enough for a lightweight 3D game character. High polish through good silhouette and clean colour shapes, not complex texture or lighting. This is a concept comparison, not a technical turnaround; avoid inconsistent perspective by using only one view per character repeated at two scales.
